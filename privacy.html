Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Flower Garden" mobile game ("the App") handles information. This App is developed by an independent individual developer.

Information We Collect

Flower Garden is primarily an offline game. Your gameplay progress (such as your best score and discovered flowers) is stored only on your own device, using your browser's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store this information on any server, and we do not have access to it.

Advertising

This App may display ads served by Google AdMob. To show relevant ads, Google and its partners may collect certain information such as your device's advertising ID, general location, and app usage data, in accordance with their own privacy practices. This data collection is performed by Google, not directly by us.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android devices).

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information directly from children. If this App is used by children, advertising shown within it is subject to Google AdMob's policies for child-directed treatment where applicable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties, other than the advertising data handled directly by Google AdMob as described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
